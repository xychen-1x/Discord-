Xychen — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Xychen collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

Xychen is an independent third-party Discord application and is not operated by or affiliated with Discord Inc.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with Xychen, we may receive information made available through Discord, such as your Discord User ID, username or display name, server ID, channel ID, and information necessary to process your commands.

When you send a message or prompt to Xychen, the content needed to process the request may be received by the Bot.

3. Conversation Storage

Xychen stores conversation data to provide features such as conversation history. Stored data may include user messages, AI-generated responses, Discord User ID, conversation/session information, and timestamps.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to process requests, generate AI responses, maintain conversation history, operate and secure Xychen, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and improve reliability and functionality.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. AI Processing — Groq

Xychen uses the Groq API to process requests and generate AI responses. Information necessary to generate a response may be transmitted to Groq. Groq's own privacy practices and policies apply to its processing.

6. Hosting — WispByte

Xychen's infrastructure is hosted using WispByte. Information stored by Xychen may therefore be processed on infrastructure provided by the hosting provider.

7. Data Retention

Conversation data is retained for the operation of conversation-history features. The specific retention period depends on Xychen's implemented storage and deletion practices.

8. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to service providers necessary to operate Xychen, including Groq for AI inference and WispByte for hosting/infrastructure. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.
